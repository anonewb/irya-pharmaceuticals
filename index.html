IryaCal-D3 combines the superior bioavailability of Calcium Citrate with the strength of Vitamin D3 to support bone health, immunity, and overall well-being.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Stronger bones & teeth with optimal calcium absorption
  • ✓ Vitamin D3 enhances calcium absorption and utilization
  • ✓ Supports muscle function and reduces cramps
  • ✓ Boosts immunity through Vitamin D3
  • ✓ Ideal for all ages — children, adults, pregnant & lactating women, and elderly
